What we keep, and why

  • Booking a table. The booking form and the booking buttons write a WhatsApp message for you, and you send it yourself. The website doesn't keep your name or number. The message says where you found us, for example Instagram, so we know which posts and ads bring people in.
  • The booking counter. When a booking message opens, the site counts it: where you found us, which night, how many guests and the time. It doesn't count who you are. We keep these counts for 13 months.
  • Adam's Club. Your name, WhatsApp number, birthday (day and month only), your stamps and visits, and where you found us. We use them to run your card and your birthday treat, and, because you ticked the box, to message you about what's on. Reply STOP to any message, or ask the team, to stop the messages. Ask us and we'll delete your card.
  • Song requests. The song, and your name and table note if you add them, so the host can play it. We delete each night's requests after 3 days.
  • The score predictor. Your picks. The table shows your first name and initial only.
  • Ask Adam. Your question goes to our AI provider so it can write the answer. We don't keep a copy linked to you, and the conversation stays on your phone until you close the page. Please don't type personal details.
  • Deposits and gift vouchers. You pay on our payment provider's secure page, so we never see your full card number. We keep what you paid for, the amount and the date, for our accounts.

What stays on your phone

The site saves a few things in your own browser, not with us: that you're over 21, your Adam's Club card link, where you first found us (for 30 days), and your chat with Adam. We don't use advertising cookies, trackers or analytics, and the fonts and map pictures come from our own website. Google Maps only opens if you tap a map link.

Who helps us run the site

We never sell your details. A few companies run parts of the site for us and only use the details to do that job: our website host, our server and database (Vercel and Upstash), our AI provider for Ask Adam (Anthropic), our card payment provider, and WhatsApp (Meta) when you message us. Some of them store data outside the UAE.

Your rights

Under the UAE's personal data protection law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), you can ask to see the details we hold about you, to correct them, to delete them, or to stop us messaging you. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply within a few days.

Over-21s only

This website features alcohol and is for adults aged 21 and over, in line with UAE law. We don't knowingly keep details of anyone younger.

Changes

If we change how we use your details, we'll update this page and the date at the top. See also our terms.
