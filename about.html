Our story

A proper local, hiding in plain sight.

Adam's Beer Kitchen JVC opened in 2026 on Floor 1 of HE Hotel Apartments. From the street you'd never know we're here. Take the lift, though, and you'll find booths, big screens, a terrace and a bar that takes its beer seriously.

We built it to be the place JVC walks to: good food that tastes better than it needs to, cold beer at fair prices, and something worth turning up for every night of the week. Quiz on Tuesday, ladies on Wednesday, steak on Thursday, karaoke on Friday, BBQ on Saturday and a proper roast on Sunday.

The people who've found us keep coming back, and they keep bringing friends. That's the plan, anyway.
